The stay

Courtyards with lily ponds, an infinity pool and 8 spacious rooms.

Leisure & lounges

Nooks and corners for slow days

Encapsulated in 5 acres of lush greenery and heritage city line, Devra has many quaint outdoor sitting arrangements and courtyards with lily ponds at every level for you to hang out with your loved ones, pets or just by yourself. The property is surrounded by farmlands and fields, giving a vast open canvas for family get-togethers, events, special occasions, team bonding activities and office get-togethers.

You will find a lot of nooks and corners to read, meditate, practise yoga or paint during your time at Devra. Each floor has common spaces open on three sides that let you feel the outdoors while being inside: the central courtyard, pool, dining area and terrace.

Rooms

8 rooms with king or twin beds

The rooms at Devra are spacious and soundproof, with either a king-size double bed or twin beds. Traditional yet contemporary architecture meets selectively curated furniture, linen and handpicked art pieces.

Large bay windows and living areas open on three sides give you the feel of being outdoors, with fresh air and sunlight, while you are indoors.

  • Air conditioning
  • Electric kettle with tea & coffee supplies
  • Telephone and writing desk
  • Large bay windows
  • Attached bathroom
  • 24x7 running hot water
  • Space for an extra bed

Facilities & amenities

Modern comfort, rustic décor

Devra offers multiple modern amenities for your comfort amidst nature and heritage-rich rustic décor. It has an infinity pool with a scenic view of the Udaipur city line for you to relax and unwind.

Same-day laundry and taxi services are available, and airport and railway station pickup and drop can be arranged on prior booking. Free Wi-Fi lets you take a staycation and keep up with your remote work schedule.

  • Infinity pool with city-line view
  • Air-conditioned rooms
  • Free Wi-Fi
  • Same-day laundry
  • Taxi service
  • Airport & railway station pickup on prior booking

Perfect for

Couples, families, pets and solo travellers

Devra is perfect for couples, families with pets, friends and family get-togethers, business leisure, solo travellers, groups of friends, weekend getaways and tourists. Its doors are always open if you are looking for some quiet time away from the hustle of daily life, or if you want to explore Udaipur and retire comfortably at the end of the day.

Devra takes pride in being safe and friendly for solo women travellers from across the globe, and is a great place for a staycation where you can work while cradled in nature. Travelling with your furry companions? Devra is a pet-friendly homestay, so they can come along too.
