Devra Udaipur

Luxury homestay and boutique stay in Udaipur that feels like home

Looking for a luxury homestay in Udaipur that feels like a private country home rather than a hotel? Devra is a boutique homestay set on a small hillock in a green estate of five acres, only 10–15 minutes from Udaipur city centre. It has eight spacious rooms, an infinity pool with a view of the city line, courtyards with lily ponds and a 360-degree view of the Aravalli hills, the Sajjan Garh fort and an 800-year-old temple.

Devra is hosted by Major Durga Das (ex-Indian Army) and Jyoti Jasol, who live on the property and look after every guest personally. In the local language, ‘Devra’ means ‘a tribal temple where God resides’, and the house runs on the old Indian idea of hospitality, Atithi Devo Bhava: guests are gods.

A boutique homestay, not a hotel

With only eight rooms, Devra is small by design. There is no lobby crowd and no buffet queue: meals are cooked for you, the hosts know you by name, and every corner of the estate is yours to explore. It is the reason guests describe it as one of the best homestays in Udaipur for a quiet, personal stay.

  • Eight rooms only, so every stay gets personal attention
  • Hosts who live on the property and know Udaipur well
  • Farm-to-plate Rajasthani food, cooked to your taste
  • Handpicked art, block-printed linen and traditional furniture
  • Five acres of gardens, courtyards and farmland to wander

Premium homestay comforts

Devra pairs rustic, heritage-rich décor with the comforts you expect from a premium homestay in Udaipur:

  • Infinity pool with a view of the Udaipur city line
  • Spacious, soundproof air-conditioned rooms with king-size or twin beds
  • Attached bathrooms with 24x7 running hot water
  • Free Wi-Fi, so you can take a staycation and keep working
  • Same-day laundry and taxi service
  • Airport and railway station pickup and drop on prior booking

Rooms with handpicked art and big bay windows

The rooms are spacious and soundproof, with either a king-size double bed or twin beds, a writing desk and space for an extra bed. Large bay windows bring in fresh air and sunlight, and the common spaces on every floor are open on three sides, so you feel the outdoors even when you are inside.

Home-cooked Rajasthani food, from farm to plate

Food is one of the main reasons guests come back. Meals are prepared in traditional Rajasthani style with organic vegetables from Devra’s own farms, supervised by Jyoti Jasol herself. North Indian dishes are always available, and food can be customised to your taste, from children’s favourites to special diets.

Things to do at Devra

  • Heritage cooking demonstrations with Jyoti Jasol
  • Yoga and meditation on the terrace, with the Aravalli hills all around
  • Morning farm walks and bicycle tours to nearby villages
  • Evenings of Rajasthani culture with the hosts

A pet-friendly homestay in Udaipur

Travelling with your dog? Devra is a pet-friendly homestay in Udaipur, and your pet will find company with the pets of Devra on the open land. It is equally loved by couples, families, groups of friends, solo women travellers, artists and anyone looking for a staycation in nature.

A luxury stay in Udaipur, 10 minutes from the lakes

A luxury stay in Udaipur usually means choosing between a large lake-view hotel in the crowded old city and a resort far outside it. Devra sits in between: close enough to reach Lake Pichola, the City Palace and the old-city bazaars in 10–15 minutes, and high enough on its hillock to wake up to birdsong, open farmland and the Aravalli hills. Spacious rooms, an infinity pool, home-cooked meals and hosts who plan your days with you make it a luxury stay without the formality of a hotel.

A boutique stay in Udaipur for couples, families and friends

If you are looking for a boutique stay in Udaipur, Devra is built around small numbers and personal attention. Couples come for quiet mornings on the terrace and sunset dinners; families come for the space, the pool and the farm walks; groups of friends book the whole house for celebrations and get-togethers. With only eight rooms, every stay feels private.

Why choose a luxury boutique homestay in Udaipur over a hotel?

  • Space: five acres of gardens, courtyards and farmland instead of a corridor of rooms
  • People: hosts who live on the property, not a rotating front desk
  • Food: home-cooked Rajasthani meals from our own farm produce, not a buffet
  • Character: handpicked art, block-printed linen and traditional furniture in every room
  • Calm: birdsong, starry skies and views of the Aravalli hills, 10–15 minutes from the city

Luxury, boutique or premium homestay: what is the difference?

A luxury homestay gives you the comfort of a good hotel (air-conditioned rooms, a pool, Wi-Fi, laundry and transfers) in a private home. A boutique homestay adds character: a small number of rooms, a house with a story, and decor chosen piece by piece. A premium homestay promises space and attention to detail. Devra is all three: eight rooms in a heritage-style home on five acres, hosted by the family who built it.

A three-day Udaipur itinerary from Devra

Day 1: Arrive and slow down. Settle into your room, take a dip in the infinity pool and watch the sun set over the Aravalli hills from the terrace, followed by a home-cooked Rajasthani dinner.

Day 2: The old city. Spend the morning at the City Palace and Jagdish Temple, walk the lanes to Bagore ki Haveli for its evening cultural show, and end the day with a sunset boat ride on Lake Pichola.

Day 3: Birds, food and forts. Start with birdwatching or a farm walk with the hosts, join a heritage cooking demonstration, then visit Fateh Sagar Lake and the Sajjangarh (Monsoon) Palace at sunset. With an extra day, drive to Eklingji Temple (22 km) or Kumbhalgarh Fort and wildlife sanctuary (82 km), whose 36 km wall is the longest in India.

How to reach our homestay in Udaipur

Udaipur is well connected by air, rail and road, and Devra can arrange pickup from the airport or the railway station on prior booking.

From Delhi and the NCR

Direct flights from Delhi to Udaipur’s Maharana Pratap Airport take about an hour and a half. By rail, overnight trains such as the Mewar Express run from Delhi to Udaipur City station, and by road Udaipur is roughly 660 km from Delhi. Many guests from Delhi, Gurugram and Noida fly in for a long weekend.

From Mumbai and Pune

Mumbai has direct flights to Udaipur, which makes Devra an easy luxury escape from Mumbai and Pune for a long weekend.

From Ahmedabad and Gujarat

Udaipur is about 260 km from Ahmedabad, around four to five hours by road, so a drive to our boutique homestay is one of the most popular weekend getaways from Ahmedabad, Vadodara and the rest of Gujarat.

From Jaipur and the rest of Rajasthan

Udaipur is about 400 km from Jaipur by road, with direct trains between the two cities. Devra fits easily into a Rajasthan circuit with Jaipur, Jodhpur, Chittorgarh and Kumbhalgarh.

From Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and abroad

Fly to Udaipur directly where available, or connect through Delhi or Mumbai. International guests usually connect through Delhi or Mumbai.

Close to the lakes, away from the noise

Devra is a 7-minute drive from Udaipur city centre, about 11 km from the bus station, 12 km from Udaipur railway station and 32 km from the airport. Lake Pichola, the City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Fateh Sagar Lake and the Sajjangarh (Monsoon) Palace are all within easy reach, and you return each evening to birdsong and starry skies on the hillock.

Questions about staying at Devra

Is Devra a luxury homestay or a hotel?

Devra is a boutique, family-run luxury homestay with eight rooms on a five-acre hillock estate in Udaipur. The hosts live on the property, so you get the comfort of a premium stay with the warmth of a home.

What makes Devra a luxury stay in Udaipur?

Spacious soundproof rooms, an infinity pool with a city-line view, home-cooked Rajasthani food, five acres of gardens and courtyards, and hosts who look after every guest personally, all 10–15 minutes from Udaipur city centre.

Is Devra a good boutique stay in Udaipur for couples?

Yes. With only eight rooms, quiet corners, a terrace with Aravalli views and sunset dinners, Devra is a favourite boutique stay in Udaipur for couples and honeymooners.

How far is Devra from Udaipur city centre and the airport?

Devra is 10–15 minutes (about a 7-minute drive) from Udaipur city centre, about 12 km from Udaipur railway station and 32 km from Maharana Pratap Airport.

How many rooms does Devra have?

Devra has eight spacious, soundproof rooms with either a king-size double bed or twin beds, attached bathrooms, air conditioning and space for an extra bed.

Is Devra a pet-friendly homestay?

Yes. Devra is a pet-friendly homestay in Udaipur, and pets are welcome on the open land of the estate. Please tell us about your pet when you book.

Does Devra have a swimming pool?

Yes, Devra has an infinity pool with a view of the Udaipur city line.

What food is served at Devra?

Home-cooked traditional Rajasthani food made with organic vegetables from our own farms, along with North Indian dishes. Food can be customised to your taste and dietary needs.

Can you arrange airport or railway station pickup?

Yes, pickup and drop from Udaipur airport and railway station can be arranged on prior booking. Taxi service is also available for sightseeing.

Is Devra safe for solo women travellers and families?

Yes. Devra takes pride in being safe and friendly for solo women travellers from across the globe, and it is a favourite with families, couples and groups of friends.

How do I reach Udaipur from Delhi?

Direct flights from Delhi to Udaipur take about an hour and a half. Overnight trains such as the Mewar Express also run from Delhi, and Udaipur is roughly 660 km from Delhi by road.

When is the best time to visit Udaipur?

October to March is the most pleasant time to visit Udaipur. The monsoon months, roughly July to September, are green and the lakes fill up, which many guests love too.
