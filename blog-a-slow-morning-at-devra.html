A slow morning at Devra

Birdsong, warm light and a walk through the farms: how a typical day begins on the hillock.

By Team Devra

A typical day at Devra starts with chirping birds and mellow, warm sunlight knocking at your door. Take your morning tea or coffee outside, where the fresh air comes straight off the fields and the Aravalli hills sit on the horizon.

Walk the farms with your hosts

Major Durga Das and Jyoti Jasol love to join guests on their morning walks through the farms. If you are travelling with a pet, it will find company with the pets of Devra while you watch them play in the open land.

Birds, yoga and quiet corners

Major is a keen bird watcher and will gladly take you out for a birdwatching session. Prefer something slower? Yoga and meditation on the terrace are a favourite with guests, and there are plenty of nooks to read, paint or simply sit.

Devra translates to a tribal temple where God resides. The house lives by Atithi Devo Bhava: guests are gods.

When you are ready for the city, Udaipur's centre is only 10 to 15 minutes away.
