Culinary

Farm to plate, supervised by Jyoti

The food at Devra is a prime attraction: tasty, appetising and made with organic vegetables from our own farms. A farm-to-plate approach means fresh fruits, vegetables and spices, free from toxic chemicals. You will witness and eat authentic Rajasthani cuisine made with love and supervised by Jyoti Jasol herself.

Apart from Rajasthani cuisine, there is a variety of North Indian dishes, and food can be customised to your taste and requirements. Kitchen regulars like pasta, noodles and sandwiches are available on request. Most of the spices are processed by locals at the property itself, and every dish tells the story of Rajasthani food and how it is produced.

Art, craft & community

Local hands, solar power

Major Durga Das and Jyoti Jasol have not only opened their doors to travellers from across the globe but also created employment and empowerment for local village women, whom you will often see at work during your stay. The property's electricity comes entirely from solar power, a step towards sustainable hospitality and a smaller carbon footprint.

Many local artisans nearby make arts and crafts indigenous to the area. You can support them by buying their work and carrying a souvenir back for yourself or your loved ones.
