About Devra

A tribal temple where God resides

Set on a small hillock amidst a lush green estate spread over 5 acres, Devra is a rustic, quaint and tranquil boutique homestay in Udaipur, merely 10-15 minutes away from the city centre. It allows you quick access to all the major tourist attractions while keeping you away from the city hustle. It has an immersive 360-degree panoramic view of the picturesque Aravalli hills, farmlands, the Sajjan Garh fort and an 800-year-old temple.

In the local language, ‘Devra’ translates to ‘a tribal temple where God resides’. True to its name, Devra believes in the core value of Indian hospitality, “Atithi Devo Bhava”, which literally means guests are gods. This pet-friendly homestay in Udaipur offers 8 rooms with all modern amenities. The refreshing country property has many nooks and corners for you to relax, read a book, paint or simply reflect, making it perfect for couple getaways, family vacations, staycations and artist retreats. Devra is also a great budget homestay in Udaipur, offering an affordable and authentic experience amidst natural beauty.

The hosts

Major Durga Das and Jyoti Jasol

Devra, the unique country homestay, is hosted by Major Durga Das (ex-Indian Army), fondly known as Major, and his wife Jyoti Jasol. The couple has been married for 30 years and belongs to the royal ancestral lines of The House of Bassi, Udaipur and the Rathores of Marwar, Jodhpur, respectively. Both Major and Jyoti are animal lovers and like to spend time with their guests and their pets, taking care of them personally during their stay. They will happily accompany you, whether going for morning walks in the farms or spending an evening getting to know Udaipur and Rajasthani culture.

Their hospitality is deeply rooted in their large joint family backgrounds, and both have been associated as culture consultants with leading hotel groups in the country, like the Oberoi group and the Udaipur palace, for their events and public relations. Growing up, Jyoti witnessed and learnt the traditional cuisines, and she thrives on sharing them through her food for the guests at Devra. She often conducts heritage cooking classes on appointment, offering a true cultural stay in Udaipur.
