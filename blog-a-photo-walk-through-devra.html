A photo walk through Devra

From the garden to the rooms, the lounges and the kitchen: a walk through Devra in pictures.

By Team Devra

Devra sits on a small hillock in a green estate of five acres, 10–15 minutes from Udaipur city centre. A short walk through the house, in pictures.

The estate

Farmland all around, the Aravalli hills on the horizon and an infinity pool looking out over the city line.

Courtyards and gardens

Every level has a courtyard or a garden corner. On fine mornings, breakfast is laid out in the garden.

Lounges for slow days

Lounges open on three sides, with handpicked art, deep sofas and old wooden furniture.

The rooms

Eight rooms: six with a double bed and two with twin beds. Some have four-poster beds, some a bay-window day bed.

The kitchen

Food is cooked the traditional Rajasthani way, with vegetables from our own farm.

We look forward to hosting you.
