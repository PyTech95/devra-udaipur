A photo walk through Devra

From the view above the hillock to the four-poster rooms, the lounges and the mud kitchen: a walk through Devra in pictures.

By Team Devra

Devra sits on a small hillock in a green estate of five acres, 10–15 minutes from Udaipur city centre. Here is a walk through the house, from the garden to the kitchen.

The estate from above

From above you can see how the house sits among the trees, with farmland all around and the Aravalli hills on the horizon. The infinity pool looks out over the city line.

Courtyards and gardens

Every level has a courtyard or a garden corner, with lily ponds and quiet places to sit. On fine mornings, breakfast is laid out in the garden by the stone house.

Lounges for slow days

Each floor has common spaces open on three sides: lounges with handpicked art, deep sofas and old wooden furniture, made for reading, painting or simply doing nothing.

The rooms

There are eight rooms, spacious and soundproof, with king-size or twin beds. Some have four-poster beds, others have window seats looking onto the green.

The kitchen

Food is cooked in traditional Rajasthani style with vegetables from our own farms, supervised by Jyoti Jasol, and some snacks still come straight off the chulha, the traditional mud stove.

We look forward to hosting you.
